Help

Tips for good clips
  • Hold the phone about 30 cm from the baby.
  • Use a quiet room. Turn off the fan or TV if you can.
  • Add clips from different days to each baby need.
  • Add a clip to a baby need only when you are sure what helped.
  • If the microphone is blocked, record in your recorder app. Then use Pick a recording.

About

An experiment. Not medical advice. If you are worried about your baby, speak to a doctor.

On 457 open cry recordings labeled by parents, this method did not beat chance. Your own clips can give a different result. The Results tab shows how it does for you.

Sound model: YAMNet (Apache 2.0), run on this device with TensorFlow.js. Clips and results stay on this device.
